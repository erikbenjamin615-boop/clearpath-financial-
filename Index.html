Important Consumer Disclosure & Disclaimer

ClearPath Financial is an independent referral and appointment scheduling service connecting interested consumers with third-party funding providers and financial service networks. ClearPath Financial is not a direct lender, does not broker loans directly, does not make credit decisions, and does not charge consumers any fees for this inquiry service.

Submitting an inquiry through this site or contacting our representatives does not guarantee loan approval or matching with a lender. Loan terms, interest rates, APR, loan amounts, and approval are determined solely by participating independent lenders based on their proprietary underwriting criteria, state licensing requirements, and applicant profile.

State Restrictions: Not all loan products are available in all states. Services are subject to specific state laws and rate limitations. Residents of New York, for example, are subject to NY state usury laws prohibiting certain high-interest small-dollar loans; alternate regulated products or credit counseling resources may be provided where applicable.

Communication & Opt-Out: By contacting us or sending an inquiry, you consent to receive direct communications regarding your request. You may opt out of SMS or WhatsApp communications at any time by replying STOP or messaging 'UNSUBSCRIBE'.
